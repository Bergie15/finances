Data

Your data folder

Everything lives in two plain CSV files: transactions.csv and budgets.csv. Nothing is ever uploaded — this page has no server.

Pick a folder on your computer and the app will read and write the CSV files there automatically every time you make a change.

Your browser can't write directly to a folder (that needs Chrome, Edge, or another Chromium browser). Your data is kept in this browser between visits — use Download below to save your CSV files, and Open to load them back.
